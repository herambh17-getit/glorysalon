Bridal makeup questions

Do you do bridal trials?

Yes. Bridal makeup trials can be booked at both Ashokvan and Shantivan. Message us your wedding date and we will fix one.

How early should I book bridal makeup?

As soon as your wedding date is fixed, especially for dates in the wedding season. That leaves time for a trial and for any skin or hair appointments you want beforehand.

Do you do engagement and party makeup?

Yes, at Ashokvan. Engagement, party and family makeup can be booked there. Shantivan offers bridal makeup.

Can I book my hair and nails at the same salon?

Yes. Ashokvan offers haircuts, colour, highlights and nail art. Shantivan offers hair spa, keratin, extensions, gel polish and nail extensions.

Do I need to book an appointment for bridal makeup?

Yes. We work by appointment only. Message us on WhatsApp or call: Ashokvan +91 99674 43305, Shantivan +91 84258 84336.

What are your timings?

Both branches are open every day, including Sundays, from 10:30 am to 8:30 pm.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard and NFC tap-to-pay.
