What a HydraFacial is

A HydraFacial is a multi-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts and hydrates the skin with a serum in one sitting. Many people book it before an event for a fresher look.

HydraFacial or a regular facial?

A regular facial is a hands-on cleanse and massage chosen for your skin type. A HydraFacial uses a device for the cleansing and serum steps. Tell us your skin concerns and we will suggest which suits you.

At Ashokvan we also offer acne and anti-ageing treatments if you want a plan beyond a single facial.

How to book hydrafacial

  1. Message a branch on WhatsApp and tell us your skin concerns.
  2. We suggest a facial or HydraFacial that suits your skin.
  3. Book your slot. We work by appointment only.

HydraFacial questions

Is there downtime after a HydraFacial?

HydraFacial is generally a no-downtime treatment, but skin reacts differently from person to person. Tell us about sensitive skin or recent treatments when you book.

What is the difference between a facial and a HydraFacial?

A facial is a hands-on cleanse and massage. A HydraFacial uses a device to cleanse, extract and infuse a serum. We can suggest which suits your skin.

Which branch offers HydraFacial?

Both Glory Ashokvan and Glory Shantivan. Acne and anti-ageing treatments are offered at Ashokvan.
