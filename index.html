Glory Beauty Salon Skinn Aesthetics and Healings is a women-led, ladies-only salon in Borivali East, Mumbai, open since 2019. It has two branches: Ashokvan, for skin, laser and bridal makeup, and Shantivan, for hair, nails and lashes. Both are open daily from 10:30 am to 8:30 pm, by appointment.

Started by Aparna in Ashokvan, now two salons in Borivali East

Glory opened in Ashokvan in 2019 as a ladies-only salon run by women. Aparna built it around skin care, laser hair removal and bridal makeup, and the Ashokvan branch still leads on those.

In 2025 we opened a second salon on Shantivan Road with more space for hair and nail work: keratin, hair Botox, extensions, gel polish and nail extensions. Both branches keep the same hours and the same ladies-only promise.
