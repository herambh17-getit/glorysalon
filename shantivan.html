Questions about our Shantivan salon

Where is the Shantivan salon located?

Glory Shantivan is at Shop No. 3, Building No. 2, Sai Ashish CHS, Shantivan Road, opposite Ram Krishna Medical, near Pangat Hotel, Nensey Colony, Borivali East, Mumbai 400066.

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 84258 84336. We work by appointment only.

Do you take walk-ins?

We work by appointment only. Keratin, smoothening, hair extensions and nail extensions can take a few hours, and booking keeps that time free for you. WhatsApp or call +91 84258 84336 to pick a slot.

What are your timings?

Open every day, Sundays included, from 10:30 am to 8:30 pm.

Do you do bridal trials?

Yes, bridal makeup trials can be booked at Shantivan. WhatsApp us your wedding date to fix one. You can plan a hair spa, nail extensions and lashes here in the same week before the wedding.

Is laser hair removal available at Shantivan?

Yes. Laser hair removal is available at this branch as well as at Ashokvan.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard and NFC tap-to-pay.

Keratin, smoothening or hair Botox: which one should I choose?

Smoothening gives the straightest result. Keratin mainly controls frizz and keeps some natural movement. Hair Botox conditions and repairs without straightening. Show us your hair and we will recommend the right one.

How long do nail extensions last?

Usually a few weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow and how you use your hands. Come back for a refill as the gap at the base grows out, rather than pulling them off at home.
