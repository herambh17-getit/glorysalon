What laser hair removal does

Laser hair removal uses concentrated light to reduce hair growth over time. It lowers how much hair grows back and how thick it is, rather than removing everything in one visit.

Hair grows in cycles, so treatment is done as a series of sessions spaced a few weeks apart. How many you need depends on the area, your hair and your skin.

Laser hair removal questions

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser gives long-term reduction in hair growth, not a guarantee that every hair is gone for good. Some people need occasional top-up sessions, and results vary with hair, skin and hormones.

How many laser sessions will I need?

Laser works over a course of sessions spaced a few weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles. The number depends on the area, your hair and your skin, so we suggest a plan after a consultation.

Can I wax or pluck between laser sessions?

Ask us before your first session. In general, waxing and plucking pull out the hair root that the laser targets, so they are usually avoided between sessions, while shaving is fine.
