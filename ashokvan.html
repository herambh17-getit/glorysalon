Questions about our Ashokvan salon

Where is the Ashokvan salon located?

Glory Ashokvan is at Shop No. 002, Shiv Darshan CHS, Shiv Vallabh Road, opposite Patel Samaj Hall, Ashokvan, Borivali East, Mumbai 400066, across the road from Sheth Midori. Look for the GLORY signboard.

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 99674 43305. We work by appointment only.

Do you take walk-ins?

We work by appointment only, so every client gets her full time slot. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 99674 43305 to book, even for a quick threading or wax.

What are your timings?

We are open every day, including Sundays, from 10:30 am to 8:30 pm.

Do you do bridal trials?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your wedding date and we will fix a trial so you can see and adjust your look before the day.

Is laser hair removal available at Ashokvan?

Yes. Laser hair removal is one of the main services at this branch, for face and body.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard and NFC tap-to-pay.

How early should I book my bridal makeup?

As soon as your wedding date is fixed, especially for dates in the wedding season. That leaves time for a trial and for any skin prep facials you want in the weeks before.

How many laser sessions will I need?

Laser works over a course of sessions spaced a few weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles. The number depends on the area, your hair and your skin, so we will suggest a plan after a consultation.
